01 / About

I’m a chemistry undergraduate at King Mongkut’s University of Technology Thonburi, working at the boundary between molecules and living systems.

My training spans supramolecular chemistry, bacteriology and protein engineering, across labs at KMUTT, Prince of Songkla University and Mahidol University. Right now I am isolating and characterizing bacteriophages as candidates for targeted drug delivery, and I co-authored a submitted study on soy-protein scaffolds for cell-based meat.

I want to build a research career in drug delivery and biomolecular design, and I’m drawn to problems that no single discipline can solve alone.
